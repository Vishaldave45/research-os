# ResearchOS Master Roadmap

## Phase 1: Traceability Core (Current Target)
- User Authentication & Session Security (JWT + Refresh Rotation).
- Workspace Isolation & Memberships.
- Core 7-Node Entity Schema (Question, Paper, Gap, Hypothesis, Experiment, Result, Decision).
- Polymorphic Relationship Engine (`Relationship` table).
- Full Evidence-Chain Query Engine & Graph API.
- Spatial Research Map (React Flow + Custom Research Nodes).
- Contextual Node Inspector & Precision Entity Views.
- Global Command Palette (`Cmd+K`) & Keyword Search.
- Seed Dataset: Efficient Deep Learning for Wireless Capsule Endoscopy (WCE).

## Phase 2: Research Workflow & Precision Auditing
- Rich experiment configuration & dataset lineage metadata.
- Timeline & audit history tracking for decisions and hypothesis pivots.
- Research health & bottleneck indicators.

## Phase 3: Integrations Layer (Adapters)
- GitHub (commit/branch linking).
- Zotero (reference metadata synchronization).
- MLflow & Weights & Biases (run/metric hydration).
- Google Drive (dataset & model checkpoint linking).

## Phase 4: Compute Adapters
- Google Colab & Kaggle metadata adapters.
- Interactive execution recording.

## Phase 5: Evidence-Aware AI Copilot
- Contextual LLM synthesis grounded strictly in the research graph.
- Citation generation with exact node back-references.

## Phase 6: Research Intelligence
- Semantic search & Graph RAG.
- Contradiction detection & unsupported claim analysis.

## Phase 7: Publication Workspace
- Overleaf integration & Claim-to-Evidence publication mappings.
